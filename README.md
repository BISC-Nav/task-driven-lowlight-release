# Task-Driven Low-Light SLAM

Comparison-method implementations and model download references, together with the proposed method's training configuration.

## Contents

- [Baseline implementations and setup](baselines/README.md)
- [Official model download references](weights/README.md)
- [Proposed-method training configuration](configs/proposed_method.json)
- [Third-party licenses](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)

The experiments compare CLAHE, DarkSLAM, URRE-Net, AirSLAM, IRAF-SLAM, DROID-SLAM, Zero-DCE, SCI, Retinexformer, RetinexNet, KinD and SNR-Aware LIE.

Third-party implementations retain their original licenses. Components distributed through their authors' repositories are listed with their source URLs and recorded revisions in [baselines/versions.json](baselines/versions.json). Pretrained models are obtained from the linked providers under their respective terms.
